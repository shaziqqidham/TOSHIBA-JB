Common questions

Questions agencies usually ask

Can you quote for sebut harga or tender requirements?

Yes. Share the specification or the ePerolehan listing and we'll prepare a quotation and supporting brochures for the models that meet it.

Do we have to buy, or can we rent?

Both are possible. Many offices prefer rental or managed print so maintenance and consumables come in one predictable monthly cost. We'll explain both options during the assessment.

How do you keep confidential documents safe?

Supported e-STUDIO models offer encrypted SSD/HDD, Trusted Platform Module, anti-malware, user authentication and secure print release, so documents only print when the right officer is at the machine.

Can we track printing cost by department?

Yes. Print management software records usage by user, department or project, with quotas and reports for budget control.

Can you help digitalise our old physical files?

Yes. Our scanning service (BPO) digitises existing records, and EDMS lets officers store, search and share them securely afterwards.

Will your products work with our existing systems?

Most products support standard networks and operating systems, and many integrate with existing software. We'll check compatibility during the assessment.
